# Addendum: Review Session IA alignment, IFRS 1 precise mechanics

The review tests the exact IFRS 1 dates, which the fiche only states in general terms. The precise rules:

- **Date of transition to IFRS:** the beginning of the earliest period for which the entity presents full IFRS information in its first IFRS financial statements. It is NOT the start of the first IFRS reporting period. First IFRS statements for the year ended 31 December 2026 with two comparatives (2025 and 2024): transition = 1 January 2024. With one comparative (2025): transition = 1 January 2025.
- **Opening IFRS statement of financial position:** prepared at the date of transition.
- **Reconciliations required in the first IFRS statements:** (a) equity under previous GAAP reconciled to IFRS equity at the date of transition, (b) equity at the end of the latest period presented under previous GAAP, (c) total comprehensive income for that latest period.

**Worked example (mirrors the review):** first IFRS statements for the year ended 30 June 2026, with full IFRS comparatives for the years ended 30 June 2025 and 2024. Date of transition = 1 July 2023 (start of the earliest period presented). Equity reconciliations at 1 July 2023 and at 30 June 2025, plus the reconciliation of total comprehensive income for the year ended 30 June 2025. Note that presenting more comparative years moves the date of transition earlier, not later, and the opening IFRS balance sheet is then older too.
