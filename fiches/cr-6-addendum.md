# Addendum — Transaction costs at initial recognition (Review Session IA)

The fiche covers initial measurement at fair value but not the treatment of transaction costs, which the review tests explicitly.

- **Rule:** at initial recognition, measure at fair value. For instruments NOT classified at FVTPL, adjust for transaction costs that are incremental and directly attributable to the acquisition or issue: **ADD them to a financial asset, DEDUCT them from a financial liability**. For instruments at FVTPL, transaction costs are **expensed immediately** in profit or loss.
- **Effective-interest link:** at amortized cost, transaction costs enter the effective-interest calculation. They increase the investor's initial outlay (asset side) or reduce the borrower's net proceeds (liability side), so they are amortized through profit or loss over the instrument's life via the effective rate.
- **Scope limit:** these are transaction costs of the financial instrument itself, not general administrative overhead.

**Worked example:** a bond is acquired for €1,000,000 plus €20,000 of directly attributable fees, in a hold-to-collect business model with SPPI cash flows. Initial carrying amount = €1,020,000 and the effective interest rate is computed on €1,020,000. If the same entity had ISSUED the bond, the liability would start at €1,000,000 − €20,000 = €980,000. If the bond were held for trading (FVTPL), the €20,000 would be expensed immediately and the bond measured at €1,000,000.
